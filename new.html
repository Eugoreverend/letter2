To My Sunshine ☀️

To my Sunshine,

In a world that can sometimes feel cold and quiet, somehow, I found someone who brings a little warmth wherever she goes.

It’s only been three months... just a small moment compared to the lifetime that stretches endlessly ahead of us. Yet somehow, in that short amount of time, you've become one of the first thoughts that crosses my mind when morning comes.

There's something about you that reminds me of dawn. Not because you're loud or overwhelming, but because you arrive gently— slowly turning the darkness into something beautiful.

Your smile feels like sunlight through a window after a long night. Your presence feels like that quiet moment when the sky changes color and you realize that a new day has finally begun.

I've tried to say these things countless times, but whenever I do, the words seem to disappear. So maybe writing them down is easier.

You have become a warmth I didn't know I was looking for. A little light in ordinary days. A reason to smile at my phone. A thought that somehow makes even the simplest moments feel special.

I don't know what tomorrow will bring. Maybe our paths will continue to grow closer. Maybe they'll wander somewhere unexpected. But if you ever find yourself wondering whether you've made someone's world brighter...

You have.

Mine.

And if you ever feel like walking beneath the morning sky with me, I'd be happy to walk beside you.

No promises about where the road leads. No pressure. Just two people, watching the sunrise together, and seeing where the next day takes us.

Yours Truly,
Kael ☀️
