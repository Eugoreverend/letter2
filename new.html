Dear Shiena, ☀️

To a ray of sunshine,

There are some things I’ve wanted to tell you for a while, but I never really had the courage to say them in person. Since I’ve resigned, I realized that I had the chance to say all of this before I left, but when the moment came, the words just wouldn’t escape my chest. I kept thinking about how I should say everything I wanted to say, but somehow, I could never find the courage to let the words out. So instead of letting everything I wanted to say remain unsaid, I figured I’d express myself in a way that feels more natural to me through writing. And rather than simply writing a normal letter, I wanted to make this a little more unique. Something you could keep, read whenever you want, and hopefully remember as a small part of the time we got to know each other. So here I am, finally saying the things I couldn’t bring myself to say in person.

First, I just want to say thank you.

Thank you for the time I got to spend with you and with the QA's. Even though the time we knew each other was brief, I’m genuinely grateful that I got the chance to meet all of you. You guys are amazing people, and I’m really glad that I got to know you, even if it was only for a short while. I know we didn’t have that much time together, but somehow, the little moments we shared became something I appreciated more than I expected. I’ll always be thankful for the memories, conversations, laughs, and the time I got to spend with such wonderful people. Meeting all of you, even for a short period, was something I’ll remember fondly.

And there’s something I want to be honest about.

I like you.

I like you not just because you’re beautiful, but because of the person you are. I like the way you work hard, the way you put effort into what you do, and how dedicated you are toward the things you want to achieve.

Honestly, seeing how hard you work made me want to work harder on myself too. It made me want to triple the effort toward my own goals. You probably never realized it, but watching you pursue the things you wanted reminded me that I should keep pushing myself too.

That’s one of the things I admire about you.

I’m telling you this not because I’m expecting you to feel the same way. I’m not asking you for an answer, a chance, or anything in return. I don't want this letter to become something that makes you feel like you owe me something.

I just wanted you to know.

Maybe these feelings will stay as nothing more than a quiet part of my memories. Maybe someday they’ll fade. And that’s okay. I’m not writing this because I need something to happen between us. I’m writing it because I didn’t want to leave without being honest about how I felt.

I’m thankful that I met you, even if it was only for a short time.

Thank you for the conversations, the moments, the laughs, and even the little things you probably don’t remember. Thank you for unknowingly becoming someone who inspired me to push myself harder.

You may never feel the same way about me, and I completely understand that. I’ll respect whatever you feel, whatever you choose, and whatever direction life takes us.

I just hope that when you read this, you know that there was someone who genuinely admired you, not only for how beautiful you are on the outside, but for the beautiful person you showed yourself to be.

I don’t need anything from you.

I just needed the courage to finally say:

I like you.

And even if that’s where this confession ends, I’ll still be thankful that I got to know you.

Take care of yourself, and keep working toward the things you dream of. I’ll be quietly cheering for you from wherever life takes me.

Thank you for being someone worth admiring.

Yours Truly,
Rod Michael ☀️
